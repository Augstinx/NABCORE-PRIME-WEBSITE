Who We Are

Protecting Industrial Assets Through Engineering Excellence

NABCORE PRIME LIMITED provides industrial maintenance, asset protection and engineering support solutions for businesses operating in demanding industrial environments.

Our services are designed to help clients protect critical equipment and infrastructure, improve asset longevity and maintain dependable operational performance.

We provide professional surface preparation, protective coating, scaffolding, insulation, industrial equipment rental and labour outsourcing services across sectors including industrial operations, oil & gas, mining and manufacturing.

Our approach combines technical capability, disciplined execution and a strong commitment to quality, safety and reliability.
