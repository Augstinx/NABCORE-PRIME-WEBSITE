Dependable Industrial Services

NABCORE PRIME LIMITED provides specialized industrial services designed to support asset protection, maintenance, construction and operational requirements.

01

Surface Preparation

Professional abrasive blasting and surface preparation services for steel structures, storage tanks and industrial facilities.

Proper surface preparation is a critical stage in industrial asset protection. Our services support the preparation of surfaces before protective coating and maintenance work.

  • Abrasive blasting
  • Steel surface preparation
  • Storage tank preparation
  • Industrial facility preparation
  • Preparation for protective coating systems
02

Protective Coating

Industrial coating systems designed to protect assets against corrosion, environmental exposure and demanding operating conditions.

Protective coating forms an important part of asset preservation by providing a protective barrier between industrial surfaces and their operating environment.

  • Industrial protective coating
  • Corrosion protection
  • Steel structure coating
  • Storage tank coating
  • Maintenance coating support

Scaffolding Services

Safe and reliable scaffolding solutions supporting industrial maintenance, construction and engineering activities.

Our scaffolding capability provides practical access solutions for work carried out on elevated structures and industrial facilities.

  • Industrial scaffolding
  • Maintenance access solutions
  • Construction access support
  • Engineering project support
  • Scaffold dismantling and related support

Insulation Services

Industrial insulation services supporting energy efficiency, temperature management and equipment performance.

Proper insulation can help industrial facilities manage heat transfer and support the performance and efficiency of equipment and systems.

  • Equipment insulation

  • Thermal protection support
  • Maintenance insulation services

Industrial equipment rental solutions supporting maintenance, surface preparation, coating and other project activities.
