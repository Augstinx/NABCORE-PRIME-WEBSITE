Strategic Partnership

Partnership with MIM-MAK Makina

Reliable LPG and fuel storage solutions through strategic industrial partnership.

NABCORE PRIME LIMITED collaborates with MIM-MAK Makina, a manufacturer in the pressure vessels industry, to provide reliable LPG and fuel storage solutions.

Established in 1994, MIM-MAK Makina has developed strong expertise in LPG and fuel tank manufacturing, becoming a trusted supplier in international markets.

The company exports to approximately 44 countries and operates across a total area of 50,000 m², including 22,000 m² of closed production facilities.

MIM-MAK Makina manufactures products according to internationally recognized standards including AD 2000, ASME and European standards.

Through this cooperation, NABCORE PRIME LIMITED is pleased to offer solutions including transport tanks, storage tanks, skid systems, dispenser systems, scale systems and fuel tanks.
