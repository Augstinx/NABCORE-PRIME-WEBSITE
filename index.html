Strategic Partnership with MIM-MAK Makina

NABCORE PRIME LIMITED is collabrates with MIM-MAK Makina, a leadering manufactureer in the pressure vessels industry, to provide reliable LPG and fuel storage solutions.

Established in 1994, MIM-MAK Makina has developed a strong expertise in LPG and fuel tank manufacturing, becoming a trusted supplier in international markets.

The company exports to approximately 44 countries and operates across a total area of 50,000 m², including 22,000 m² of closed production facilities, supported by strong production capacity and advanced engineering capabilities.

MIM-MAK Makina manufactures products according to the internationally reconized standards including AD 2000, ASME and European standards, ensuring rrliable solutions for different project requirements.

Through this cooperation, NABCORE PRIME LIMITED is pleased to offer high-quality solutions includimg transport tanks, storage tanks (abovegroung and underground), skid systems, dispenser systems, scale systems and fuel tanks.
