About NABCORE PRIME

Protecting industrial assets through engineering excellence and innovative industrial solutions.

NABCORE PRIME LIMITED was established to provide reliable industrial maintenance and asset protection solution. We help businesses extend the lifespan of critical equipment and infrasstructure through high-quality workmanship, innovation solutions and dependable servies.

We provide professional surface preparation, protective coating, scaffolding, insulation, industrial equipment rental and labour outsourcing services for the industrial, oil & gas, mining and manufacturing sectors.

We are committed to delivering high-quality services that meet the unique needs of our clients, ensuring the protection and longevity of their industrial assets.
